Until now MateCrew assumed one fridge held one product. Offices stock several, so
everything that used to be a single number is now tracked per product.

Every office has one **default** product, used whenever a flow does not name one
— that is what keeps older history intact and what "take a can" falls back to.

## For admins

Manage the catalogue under **Admin → Items**. You can add a product, retire one
without losing its history, reorder them, and set which is the default.

Each product also carries its own nutrition figures — volume, sugar, caffeine —
which is what makes the health side of the statistics meaningful once you stock a
zero-sugar option next to a regular one.

<Callout>
  Purchases are recorded per line, so one order can contain several products at
  different prices. That is what makes per-product billing possible.
</Callout>
