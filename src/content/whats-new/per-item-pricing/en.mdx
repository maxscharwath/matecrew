Once offices stocked several products at different prices, splitting the bill by
head stopped being fair. Reimbursements now bill **per product**.

## How it works

Each product gets its own price: the weighted average of what was actually paid
for it, counting only purchases made up to the end of the period.

<Compare
  beforeLabel="Before"
  afterLabel="Now"
  before="One average price for everything. Drink only the cheap one, pay the same as someone on the expensive one."
  after="Your cost is the sum, per product, of how many you drank × that product's price."
/>

Whoever paid is credited **per product** too, in proportion to what they spent on
it. Over the full run of a purchase, a payer is credited back exactly what they
put in — no money appears or disappears in the split.

## Periods stay stable

A period's prices are capped at its end date, so recording a bulk order in
September never reshuffles August. Once a period is generated its payment lines
are frozen.

If something genuinely needs correcting — a late invoice, a wrong consumption —
an admin can recalculate the period. Lines already marked paid are left alone and
their amounts are deducted from the remaining balances, so nobody is asked to pay
twice.

<Callout>
  Admins can preview a month before generating it, which shows each person's
  share, the per-product prices used, and the transfers that would be created —
  without saving anything.
</Callout>

There is also a price-history chart on the purchases screen, so you can see what
maté has been costing over time.
