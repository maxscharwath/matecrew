## Your own figures

Because each product carries its own volume, sugar and caffeine, your totals turn
into something meaningful: litres drunk, grams of sugar, milligrams of caffeine,
and a per-day average.

Those averages are shown against published reference values, so a rating of
"moderate" or "high" means something specific rather than a number we invented.

<Meter
  label="Sugar, average per day"
  value={31}
  unit=" g"
  ideal={25}
  max={50}
  state="above the ideal"
/>

<Meter
  label="Caffeine, average per day"
  value={168}
  unit=" mg"
  ideal={200}
  max={400}
  state="moderate"
/>

The marks are the WHO free-sugar guidance and the EFSA daily caffeine limit. They
count only the maté you drank at the office, so treat them as a rough signal
rather than a medical opinion.
