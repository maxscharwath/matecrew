Pick the window you care about: this week, 30 or 90 days, the last 12 months,
year to date, or everything.

## Your own figures

Because each product carries its own volume, sugar and caffeine, your totals can
be turned into something meaningful: litres drunk, grams of sugar, milligrams of
caffeine, and a per-day average.

Those averages are shown against published reference values — the WHO free-sugar
guidance and the EFSA daily caffeine limit — so a rating of "moderate" or "high"
means something specific rather than being a number we invented.
