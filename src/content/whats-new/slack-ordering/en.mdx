When a maté session opens, MateCrew posts to the office channel. That message is
now interactive — pressing the button registers you on the spot, with no browser
redirect.

## Linking your account

The first time you press the button, MateCrew matches your Slack email to your
MateCrew account and links the two automatically. If the emails differ, you get a
private message with a **Connect** button that opens a one-time link to confirm —
after that it just works.

## When the session closes

At the cutoff, MateCrew posts a summary with a **mark all served** button, so
whoever fetched the round can close it out without leaving Slack. That records
everyone's consumption and deducts the cans from stock.
